About

Built to ship.

I'm a full-stack developer who cares about the parts users never see — the load time, the failure case, the thing that still works on a bad connection. Most of what I build is plain, fast and boring underneath, which is what lets the surface be interesting.

I work end to end: shaping the idea, building the thing, putting it in front of people, then fixing what the first week reveals. I'd rather ship something small that's genuinely finished than something large that's perpetually almost done.
