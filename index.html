01 About

Building things that hold up.

I'm a software engineer who likes taking systems apart to see how they work, then putting them back together a little better than I found them. My focus is the full stack — from data models and APIs to the pixels people actually click on.

I care about software that's boring in the best way: predictable, well-tested, and easy for the next person to pick up. Outside of client work I spend my time on side projects, reading source code, and writing about what I learn.
