Rebuilding this site in the open

A quick note on why this portfolio exists, what it's built with, and what's still a work in progress.

For a while, this domain didn't point anywhere in particular — just a placeholder, waiting for a real site to catch up to it. This post marks the start of that changing.

The site itself is intentionally simple: plain HTML, CSS, and a small amount of JavaScript, with no build step and no framework to keep up to date. It's fast, it's easy to edit by hand, and it deploys anywhere that can serve static files — which turns out to be almost everywhere.

The design leans on a few ideas: warm paper tones instead of stark white, one confident accent color instead of a palette of ten, a serif for headlines paired with a monospace for labels and dates, and generous whitespace instead of clutter. The goal was something that feels considered rather than templated.

A few things here are still placeholders — the project cards, the experience timeline, the résumé details. Filling those in with real work is next, and this blog is where the progress will get written down.

If you're reading this, the site is live. Everything else is a work in progress — which, for a portfolio, feels about right.
